# Code of conduct

Openroom is built by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be kind, be patient, and assume good intent.

- Welcome newcomers and questions, including simple ones.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. No insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Do not share anyone's private information, documents or data.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, use the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) and say it is a conduct report.
